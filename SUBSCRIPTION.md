A purchase is completed through Apple. Apple charges the payment method associated with your Apple Account and supplies transaction records.

If Apple offers an eligible free trial, no subscription charge is made for the trial period. Unless you cancel before the deadline shown by Apple, the trial converts automatically to a paid yearly subscription. Apple's eligibility result controls.

## Renewal

The subscription renews automatically for another year unless cancelled before the renewal deadline shown by Apple.

## Price changes

Apple will display or notify price changes as required by its rules and applicable law. A price increase may require consent. If required consent is not given, the subscription may end at the close of the current period.

## Cancellation

Cancel in Apple Account → Subscriptions → Teslatlas → Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app, resetting data or disconnecting a source does not cancel. Access ordinarily continues to the end of the paid period unless Apple or law states otherwise.

## Restore

Use Restore Purchases in Teslatlas or Apple's restore mechanism with the Apple Account that owns the entitlement.

Apple administers App Store refunds. Mandatory consumer rights remain. Withdrawal rights for immediately supplied digital content/services depend on the applicable law, consent and Apple purchase flow; these terms do not remove mandatory remedies.

## Failed or disputed payment

Apple may suspend or end entitlement if payment fails, is reversed or refunded. A technical grace period does not create a permanent free licence.

## Scope

The subscription gives access to the current Teslatlas feature set. It does not include third-party source hosting, a vehicle account, mobile data, electricity, TeslaMate support or MyTeslaMate fees.
