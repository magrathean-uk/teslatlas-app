## 2. Product design

Teslatlas is local-first. It imports supported data into an on-device mirror and performs substantial processing locally. Magrathean does not receive vehicle-history data merely because Teslatlas is installed or because the local mirror is created.

Local-first does not mean no network activity. Data can leave the device when you connect to a source, use Apple services, enable an optional provider feature, open links or deliberately share an export.

## 3. Data processed on your device

Depending on your source and features, Teslatlas may process locally:

- vehicle identifiers, model and configuration;
- drives, routes, positions and timestamps;
- charging sessions, energy, battery and state data;
- addresses, geofences and place labels;
- cost and tariff settings;
- source endpoint and non-secret configuration;
- credentials stored using Apple Keychain;
- local preferences, diagnostics and readiness state;
- user notes;
- map and derived analytics data;
- prepared AI fact bundles and system-search items if enabled;
- subscription entitlement/cache state.

You control the device and configured source. Magrathean does not ordinarily access this local content.

## 4. Source connections

### TeslaMate PostgreSQL

Teslatlas may connect directly to a PostgreSQL database you control. Connection and source data travel between your device and that endpoint. The source operator controls server logs, backups and access.

### MyTeslaMate-compatible API

Teslatlas may send requests and credentials to the user-selected API endpoint. The provider can process account, vehicle, IP and connection data under its own privacy terms. MyTeslaMate is independent from Magrathean.

### Teslatlas Hub

Teslatlas may pair with a private Hub and send pairing/device information, bearer authentication and sync requests to that Hub. The Hub operator controls the deployment and its logs/data. If Magrathean later offers a hosted Hub, a separate hosted-service notice will apply.

## 5. Apple services

Apple can process data when you download/update Teslatlas; purchase, renew, restore or refund a subscription; use MapKit/Apple Maps; use Siri, App Intents, Spotlight/System Search or Apple Intelligence; or use iCloud/device backups and platform diagnostics.

Apple acts under its own terms and privacy policy. Magrathean receives limited transaction/entitlement information needed to provide access, but not your full payment-card details.

## 6. Optional weather context

If you enable online weather enrichment, Teslatlas sends to approved Open-Meteo endpoints:

- coordinates rounded to approximately 0.1 degrees;
- relevant dates/times and weather query parameters;
- ordinary network information such as IP address necessarily visible to the provider.

The purpose is to add weather context. The provider may retain server logs under its published terms. This feature is optional and can be disabled.

Because rounded route coordinates and IP addresses can be personal data, the App Store privacy label must be reconciled with the submitted build and provider terms.

## 7. AI and system search

AI features are optional and default off. The current implementation prepares limited vehicle facts locally and uses Apple's Foundation Models where available. The current build must not claim use of Private Cloud Compute unless that path is actually enabled and disclosed.

If a future version transmits AI inputs to a remote model, we will update this policy, App Store privacy answers and in-app consent before enabling it.

System Search indexing is separate and default off. When enabled, selected items are made available to the device's system search under Apple controls.

## 8. Exports and support

Teslatlas creates exports locally. Nothing is uploaded to Magrathean merely because an export is generated.

If you deliberately share a support bundle, email, screenshot, log or database extract with us, we process it to investigate, respond, secure the product, defend claims and improve support. Avoid sending live credentials, complete unredacted histories or unnecessary personal data.

## 9. Website and communications data

When you use our website or contact us, we may receive name/contact details, message/support content, IP/request/security logs, cookie/consent data, App Store transaction reference and legal/security report information.

The separate Magrathean website privacy notice applies to general corporate website processing. This product policy prevails for Teslatlas-specific processing.

## 10. Purposes and lawful bases

Where UK/EEA law applies:

- provide app access/support: contract;
- respond to enquiries/complaints: contract or legitimate interests;
- secure and defend the product: legitimate interests and legal obligations;
- comply with tax/accounting/law: legal obligation;
- optional communications: consent or lawful legitimate interests;
- optional weather functionality: contract/your request, with provider separately responsible under its terms;

Local device processing under your sole control may fall outside Magrathean's controller activities.

## 11. Recipients

We may disclose data we actually receive to authorised staff/contractors; hosting, email, security, professional and support providers; Apple for platform-controlled processes; regulators, courts or advisers where lawful; and a business buyer/investor under confidentiality.

We do not sell personal data and do not use Teslatlas vehicle history for third-party advertising.

## 12. International transfers

Where Magrathean controls a restricted transfer, we use an applicable adequacy regulation, UK International Data Transfer Agreement/addendum, EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful safeguard. Data sent directly to a selected source/provider is also governed by that operator/provider.

## 13. Retention

We ordinarily retain support cases up to 24 months after closure; security records for the product life plus a reasonable defence period; legal/tax/transaction records for statutory periods; and website logs according to the website schedule. We may retain evidence longer where necessary for legal claims.

Local app data remains until you delete/reset it, the OS removes it or a backup restores it. Source data remains under the source operator's retention.

## 14. Security

We use access control, encryption in transit where applicable, Keychain storage, redaction, least privilege, secure development and incident response. No method is completely secure.

## 15. Your rights

Depending on law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and review of certain automated decisions.

These rights apply to data Magrathean controls. For data held only on your device or by your selected source, use app/source controls or contact that operator. We may verify identity and apply lawful exemptions.

## 16. Children

Teslatlas is not intended for children and is not designed to collect children's data. Do not configure it to monitor another person without a valid legal basis and appropriate notice.
